# BM25 lexical ranking

BM25 is a bag-of-words ranking function. It increases a document score when query terms occur in that document, while inverse document frequency gives uncommon terms more influence than ubiquitous ones. The method does not require training data or neural inference.

The parameter k1 controls how quickly repeated term frequency saturates. The parameter b controls document-length normalization. A common baseline uses k1 equal to 1.5 and b equal to 0.75, although useful values depend on the collection.

Because BM25 operates on tokens, transparent tokenization matters. Lowercasing improves case-insensitive matching, while punctuation rules determine whether identifiers remain intact. Stable tie handling is also necessary for reproducible experiments.
