Methodology

Deliberately modest.

Twenty-four curated questions test identifiers, acronyms, paraphrases, distractors, semantic wording, and controls. All systems receive identical chunks and labels.

  • Small synthetic diagnostic benchmark
  • No LLM judge or automatic faithfulness model
  • No claim of production-scale performance
  • Category groups are not statistically powered
  • Public benchmark is distinct from the two-question integration fixture

Built as an inspectable engineering benchmark.
